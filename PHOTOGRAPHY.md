## Home refinement assignments

The eight fictional properties now have eight distinct primary photographs. None of the three featured-property secondary views doubles as another property's primary image. These pairings establish a visual identity for fictional residences; they do not assert that a primary and secondary source photograph show the same real building.

Selected Residences uses the alternate city outlook for Mont Kiara and alternate courtyard for Bangsar, retaining the established garden image for Canopy. New to Havenza uses Canopy's botanical detail and Southern Light's own blue-sofa interior. On Home, the closing photograph is exclusive to its closing section; the Rent page also uses it for an editorial pause. The approved hero photograph remains unchanged.

There are now 13 local WebP files: 12 photographs plus the mobile hero crop. Five photographs were added in the refinement pass, each under 325 KB. Supporting assets remain lazy-loaded.
